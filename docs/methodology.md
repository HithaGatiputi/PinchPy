# Methodology

1. Define hot and cold process streams with supply temperature, target temperature and heat-capacity flow rate.
2. Validate stream directions and positive heat-capacity flow rates.
3. Apply the chosen ΔTmin temperature shifts.
4. Build shifted temperature intervals.
5. Perform the heat cascade to obtain minimum hot and cold utility targets.
6. Identify the shifted pinch temperature and corresponding hot/cold temperatures.
7. Use these deterministic results as labels for ANN training.
8. Evaluate ANN predictions with MAE, RMSE and R² and compare them with the exact pinch engine.
9. Extend the validated results into heat-exchanger-network synthesis/evaluation.
